# Photography for PB-01

Drop the files below into this folder. The landing page picks them up on the
next request: each figure renders only when its file is present, so a missing
photograph leaves a clean layout rather than a broken image icon.

Nothing here is required for the page to work. Add them one at a time and
reload.

## The four slots

| File | Shape | Minimum size | What it should show |
|---|---|---|---|
| `registry.jpg` | 16:9 | 1600 x 900 | A records or registry office. Folders, drawers, a desk mid-work. The page's one wide band. |
| `faculty.jpg` | 4:3 | 1200 x 900 | The exterior or a working interior of a partner university. Something a candidate could recognise. |
| `study.jpg` | 3:2 | 1200 x 800 | Somebody reading or working. Unposed, ordinary light. |
| `convocation.jpg` | 16:9 | 1600 x 900 | A graduation, a hall, a cohort. The end of the funnel. |

`.jpg` or `.webp` both work. Use the exact filenames.

## Two rules worth keeping

**Faces need a lawful basis.** This product processes passport photographs and
teaches the law that governs them, so its own marketing page is the last place
to publish somebody's face without their agreement on the record. Either use
photographs where people are not identifiable, or hold written consent from
everyone who is. A convocation photograph taken in a public ceremony is not
automatically fair game for marketing.

**No stock.** A photograph on this page reads as *this place, these people*.
An image of a campus that is not a partner university implies facilities that
do not exist, to somebody deciding whether to pay a non-refundable application
fee. That is the reason this folder exists rather than a seeded placeholder
service.

## Weight

Students on 3G are a stated product principle. Keep each file under about
250KB; resize to the dimensions above rather than shipping a 4MB original.
The page lazy-loads everything below the fold and reserves the space, so
adding photographs will not move the layout while they load.
